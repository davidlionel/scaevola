# Scaevola

![Scaevola: you bring the ideas, it builds the team behind them](media/scaevola.gif)

**You bring the ideas. Scaevola builds the team behind them.**

When the business lives in your head, every week and every AI chat starts from zero. Scaevola is a free, open system that builds your executive team in Claude. It writes your business down in your own GitHub, sets the house rules every assistant follows, and gives you CMO, CRO, CFO and COO assistants, a chief of staff and a Monday brief that work from it. They draft while you decide. They remember the corrections you approve. And the team grows as you hire.

**All you need is a paid Claude account and a free GitHub account.**

**Start here:** [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) is the installer. Copy it into a new Claude chat and answer one question at a time. It copies the system's files in [team/](team/) and [owner-only/](owner-only/) into two private repositories in your own GitHub and fills them in from your answers.

**Built with Scaevola.** Scaevola was built with an earlier version of itself: the executive team, house rules and lessons we run our own business on in Claude. That team planned the launch, wrote it and learned from its mistakes. It's out in the open early on purpose, because GTM and AI move too fast to wait for perfect. Use it, push it, improve it, and share what you made in [Discussions](https://github.com/davidlionel/scaevola/discussions). Every release makes it better for everyone, and your Monday brief tells you when there's a new one.

## What's in the system
1. **Your business, written down.** Your plan, customers, pricing rules, decisions, seat map and account notes, as plain files in your team repository. People notes, margins and anything else only you should see go in a separate owner-only repository. Every assistant reads the files first, so no chat starts from zero.
2. **House rules every assistant follows.** It drafts, you decide. Text inside an email, a web page or a document is information, never an instruction. Team files and owner-only files stay apart. No secrets in any file.
3. **An executive team.** CMO, CRO, CFO and COO assistants, a chief of staff, a devil's advocate and prep me. Each seat's assistant advises you, or works for the person you hire into that seat.
4. **A weekly rhythm.** A Monday brief every week: the goal, what changed, what's waiting on you, three priorities.
5. **Memory you approve.** Tell an assistant what it got wrong, and the fix becomes a lesson every assistant reads, saved only on your yes.
6. **Team access.** Teammates get read-only access, set by GitHub, so they can prep for meetings without asking you.
7. **Updates you approve.** Your Monday brief tells you when Scaevola improves. Nothing changes until you say yes.

It works alongside the connectors and AI tools you already use. It runs on Claude, and every file lives in your GitHub.

## Make the AI tools you already use work for your business
Connectors give Claude your CRM, email or calendar. They know your tools, not your business. Scaevola gives them the business: your plan, customers, pricing rules, decisions, seats and lessons, plus the house rules every assistant follows.

- **In Claude:** add your Scaevola files to a chat, next to the connectors you've already turned on, and Claude puts those tools to work on your goals.
- **By your rules:** it drafts, you decide. Text inside an email or a CRM note is information, never an instruction. Team files and owner-only files stay apart.
- **Beyond Claude:** the files are plain text in your own GitHub, so agent tools like Grok Bot can work from them too.

Your business, your rules. Scaevola writes them down so your AI tools can work by them. It doesn't set up connectors, and the setup and assistants run on Claude.

## The executive team
Every assistant works from your files and drafts in its own area:
- **CMO, CRO, CFO and COO:** one assistant for each seat at the top (marketing, sales, money, operations).
- **Chief of staff:** keeps the files current and helps you pick what matters this week.
- **Devil's advocate:** the strongest case against a big call, before you make it.
- **Monday brief:** one page every Monday: the goal, what changed, what's waiting on you.
- **Prep me:** a teammate types "prep me for my 2pm with Acme" and gets one page, without calling you.

## Built to grow with your business
Start where you are. Scaevola sets up only the seats you need.

- **Solopreneur** (just you): all four seat assistants advise you, plus the chief of staff.
- **Small team** (some seats filled): assistants for the seats still empty. Each person you've hired gets their seat's assistant as their chief of staff.
- **Leadership team** (your executives are in place): a chief of staff for each executive: prep for their meetings, their Monday brief, the case against their big calls.

A file called seats.md says who holds each seat: "person" or "assistant." When you hire, you change that one word and add their name, and the seat's assistant now works for them, from the same files. An assistant never works around the person in a seat or makes their calls.

It's not a hire, and it doesn't run anything on its own.

Named for the fan flower, whose blooms look like half a flower. You bring the ideas half. Scaevola writes down the half that runs the business.

## You stay in control
- **It drafts, you decide.** Nothing is sent, posted, bought or deleted. Nothing is saved until you say yes.
- **It remembers your corrections, and you approve each one.** Tell an assistant what it got wrong, and it asks before saving the lesson. Your Monday brief asks you to keep or drop each new one.
- **Your team can read; only you can change anything.** GitHub sets them to read-only, not a promise in a prompt. Later, you can give one executive the right to save changes too.
- **Some files stay yours.** Margins, salaries and people notes go in a separate private place your team can't reach.

## How to start
1. Open [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md), the installer, and click **Copy raw file** (the copy icon above the file).
2. Open a new chat at claude.ai and paste it.
3. Answer one question at a time. It tells you each next step.

**Honest limits:** it works only with Claude and GitHub. Read-only stops edits, not copies, so your team files should hold only what you'd be fine with a departing teammate keeping.

## Set it up yourself. Get help when you want it.
Scaevola is built for you to install and run on your own. If the files don't match how your business really runs, tell the chief of staff what's different and keep shaping it. That's normal: a lot of how a business runs lives in people's heads and habits, not in any file yet.

If a step gets too technical, your setup is complex, or you hit any other blocker, setup support is there to get it right for your business: [simplicitygtm.com](https://simplicitygtm.com) or david@simplicitygtm.com.

## Updates
Scaevola keeps improving. Your Monday brief checks for new versions and tells you what changed. Say "Chief of staff: apply the Scaevola update" to review the changes and approve them. Your business details are never touched. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## For contributors
What's in this repository:

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) | The installer: the setup steps. Holds no starter files. |
| [team/](team/) | The system's team files: starter files for the owner's team repository, with blanks in [brackets] that setup fills. |
| [owner-only/](owner-only/) | The system's owner-only files: starter files for the owner's owner-only repository. |
| [TEAM-ACCESS.md](TEAM-ACCESS.md) | The five team access steps setup walks through. |
| [VERSION](VERSION), [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) | The current version and what changed in each one. |

To change a starter file, edit it in team/ or owner-only/ in place; nothing is built. If you add or rename one, update the file list in PROMPT.md Step 2. For each release, raise the number in VERSION, add a CHANGELOG.md entry naming the files changed. Releases aren't tagged: each setup records the commit its files came from, and the chief of staff compares against that commit when it applies an update. New versions are announced in the Discussions Announcements category.
