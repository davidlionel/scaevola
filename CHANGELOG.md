# What's new in Scaevola

Each update lists what changed in plain words and the files it touches. Your Monday brief checks this page and tells you when there's something new. To apply an update, open a chat and say "Chief of staff: apply the Scaevola update." Nothing changes without your yes, and your business details stay as they are.
